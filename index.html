Questions

Quick answers.

What does the minimum spend cover?

The $500 minimum goes toward food and drinks for your group. The kitchen can create a menu for budgets of all types, and the price already includes sales tax.

Can I see a layout of the rooftop?

There is no printed floor plan, but Jaxton is happy to send extra photos or hop on a quick call to walk you through the space.

Can we bring our own food, drinks or DJ?

Catering and drinks are handled in house, so outside food and BYO alcohol are not allowed. You can play your own music on the sound system, but outside DJs are not permitted.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel 7 or more days before your event for a full refund. Between 7 days and 24 hours you get 50% back. Cancellations within 24 hours are non refundable.

Is the rooftop accessible?

The rooftop is upstairs and there is no lift, so it is not wheelchair accessible. Accessible restrooms are available on site.
